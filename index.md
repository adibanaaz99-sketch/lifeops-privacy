# LifeOps: Productivity Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

LifeOps is a personal productivity app for organizing tasks and categories.

## Your data stays on your device
Your tasks, categories, notes and display name are stored **on your device** and, if enabled, in your private iCloud storage. Current local-first versions do not send this content to our account or statistics server. You may use LifeOps without an account in local-only guest mode.

## Optional iCloud sync
In versions offering Sign in with Apple, signed-in users may turn on iCloud sync; guests stay local-only. Your task data is copied to **your own private iCloud account**, protected by Apple, not our server. Sign in with Apple and the iCloud account configured on your device are separate. Sync pauses if the device's iCloud account changes or the account service is unavailable. You can turn sync off at any time. Without a synced copy or your own backup, lost/reset/deleted device data cannot be recovered.

## Your name
You may type a greeting name, skip it, change it in Settings, or allow Apple to provide your first name during optional sign-in. Your name stays on-device/private iCloud and is never sent to our account or statistics server. Earlier account-free builds offer only typed names.

## Optional Apple sign-in and account information
Versions offering Sign in with Apple use our server to verify Apple's one-time confirmation. We store a random LifeOps account ID, a hashed Apple user identifier, account timestamps and whether LifeOps iCloud data was initialized. We do not store your password, name, email, Apple access/refresh/identity tokens or the device's LifeOps session on the server. Apple tokens are processed transiently for verification and, during deletion, revocation. The LifeOps session is kept securely in your device's Keychain. The server's developer authorization key is a private server secret, not a user credential or part of the app.

Account information is processed on Render and MongoDB Atlas for app functionality until you delete the account. Signing out removes local LifeOps data and credentials, but preserves your account and private iCloud copy.

## Usage statistics (optional)
To understand usage, LifeOps sends a daily summary: random install ID, date/first-seen date, app version, platform/OS version and counts (opens, tasks created/completed, open tasks and whether sync is enabled). In signed-in versions, individual summaries are linked to your LifeOps account ID; they are not anonymous while linked. Guest summaries use a random installation ID. They never include task titles/notes, name, email, location or contacts and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can turn this off in Settings under "Share usage statistics" (older builds say "Share anonymous usage stats"). Render and MongoDB Atlas process these records on our behalf. On account deletion, linked individual records are deleted and only global, unlinked count totals remain, without account/install identifiers, dates or device details.

## Other
We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Calendar access, if you grant it, is only used to add your tasks to your calendar. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

## Data deletion
In signed-in versions, select **Settings → Delete account and data**, then confirm. This removes the LifeOps account association and linked individual usage records, local tasks/categories/profile, scheduled LifeOps reminders, credentials and LifeOps iCloud data. Unlinked aggregate count totals may be retained. If cleanup is interrupted, a retry screen pauses editing and sync until it finishes. Devices that are offline must reconnect to receive account invalidation and iCloud deletion.

LifeOps requests a fresh Apple confirmation to revoke authorization without retaining user tokens. If that is cancelled or Apple revocation is unavailable, validly authorized data deletion still proceeds and the app explains how to stop using Sign in with Apple for LifeOps in Apple Settings. An expired LifeOps session requires fresh Apple confirmation.

This does not delete your Apple ID, unrelated iCloud files or backups/calendar entries you exported outside LifeOps. Deleting the app alone does not delete a server account or necessarily remove iCloud/Keychain data; use the in-app deletion option first. Guests can delete tasks locally and contact us about installation-linked usage records. Earlier cloud-enabled builds lack account deletion; replace them with the current account-lifecycle version or contact us for help. Older releases used a server-backed task architecture; the local-first data statement describes current versions, not those earlier releases.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
